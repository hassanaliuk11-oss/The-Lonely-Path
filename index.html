I

Silent Tears

The night falls heavy, stars refuse to shine, My eyes are rivers, flowing tears of mine. Each breath is hollow, each sigh a pain, Memories linger, like an endless chain. Shadows move softly across my room, Whispering your absence, igniting gloom. I reach for comfort, but find the air bare, A void that echoes, a wound laid bare. Your name is a whisper I cannot speak, It dances in silence, makes my knees weak. The walls remember, the floor knows my grief, Every corner of this house offers no relief.

II

Broken Heart

My heart lies shattered across cold stone, Each fragment whispers I am alone. Love once burned like fire in the night, Now embers fade, leaving only fright. I walk through the echoes of what was, Haunted by absence, bound by loss. Every smile I wore now feels like a lie, Laughter turned hollow, tears I cannot hide. Your shadow lingers in every hall, A silent echo, answering my call. Moonlight cuts through the window pane, And I shiver, remembering the pain.

III

Shadow of Memories

Shadows linger where you used to stand, Memories slipping like grains of sand. I touch the air, hoping for a trace, Only emptiness greets my embrace. Nights stretch endless, a river of dark, Every heartbeat echoes a hollow mark. Your laughter haunts the corners of mind, A cruel reminder of love left behind. I wander through rooms that remember your name, Each step is weighted with sorrow and shame. Dreams show your face then pull away, Leaving me trapped in another day.

IV

Lost Dreams

Dreams we built now lie in decay, Scattered fragments of a brighter day. I reach for hope but find only dust, Shattered trust, betrayal, and rust. Nights echo with silent screams, Torn by the loss of what might have been. Every step forward feels the same, Haunted by absence, drenched in pain. The moon hides behind a veil of sorrow, No promise shines for the hope of tomorrow. Tears water the soil of regret, Harvesting grief that I cannot forget.

V

Dark Night

The night swallows the last light of day, Leaving only darkness to guide my way. Stars blink faintly, indifferent above, While my soul searches for a lost love. Each shadow stretches across the floor, Echoing the pain that I cannot ignore. The wind carries whispers of your name, Filling the silence with sorrow and blame. I lie awake, haunted and torn, Remembering the nights when hope was born. The clock ticks slowly, mocking my despair, Every moment a reminder you’re not there.

Echoes of Waiting

My soul is a desert, thirsting for grace, Lost in the absence of your smiling face. Every sunset is heavy, every dawn is a plea, To break these shackles and set my heart free. But the stars remain silent, the moon turns away, As I drift through the ghosts of another long day. The ink on the pages of our story has dried, Carrying the secrets I’ve buried inside.

Unspoken Words

We walk in the same wind, we breathe the same air, Yet the distance between us is more than I bear. A touch that was fleeting, a glance that was brief, Is the root of my joy and the source of my grief. I build you a kingdom in the world of my sleep, With promises made that I’m destined to keep. The world hears my laughter, but never my soul, Missing the pieces that once made me whole. So I’ll stay in the shadows, a witness unseen, Lost in the spaces of what might have been. Loving you quietly, without sound or sign, Knowing you’re out there, but never quite mine.

Lonely Moments

Nights stretch long, the stars seem dim, A candle flickers, then grows dim. Yet still I walk, though weary and torn, Carrying the weight of a heart forlorn. This path is cruel, yet I must remain, A traveler lost in grief and pain.

I wander this room, a ghost in its space, Haunted by love I cannot replace. And as silence settles, heavy and deep, I lie awake while the room sleeps.

One day I looked at myself
And didn’t recognize who stayed
I left eventually
But I left empty

XLVIII

Tears That Had No Witness

I cried when no one was watching
Not because I wanted privacy
Because no one stayed
My tears soaked pillows
Sleeves
Hands
They fell honestly
Unfiltered
Ugly
Real
I cried for hours
Then wiped my face quietly
Answered messages like nothing happened
Smiled carefully
Laughed on command
No one asked about my eyes
No one noticed my voice shaking
That hurt more than crying alone
These tears had no witness
But they knew the truth

XLIX

The Part of Me That Never Returned

Something left me that day
Not you
Me
I laughed later
Worked later
Lived later
But something was missing
I searched for it in memories
In people
In prayers
It never came back
That part trusted deeply
Loved honestly
Believed easily
Pain took it silently
Now I live carefully
With limits
With walls
People think I healed
They don’t see what never returned

L

When Even Tears Give Up

I cried until my eyes forgot how to beg
Tears slowed not from healing but exhaustion
My face stayed wet my heart stayed dry
I stared at the ceiling counting breaths
Each one felt borrowed
Pain sat quietly confident it would stay
I tried remembering joy
My mind returned emptiness
Names passed through me without warmth
Promises sounded foreign
I wiped my cheeks out of habit
Nothing changed
Silence grew comfortable with my breaking
Night leaned closer listening
I whispered prayers without faith
Faith did not answer
My chest hurt in places words can’t reach
I felt smaller than my sorrow
Even tears stepped back
And left me alone with everything else

LI

The Day I Buried Myself Alive

I smiled that day
People thought I was fine
Inside I was digging carefully
Layer by layer
Hope went first
Then trust
Then my voice
I buried reactions
Buried expectations
Buried my need to be seen
I learned how to nod politely
How to say “it’s okay”
How to disappear without leaving
No one attended the burial
No one noticed the grave
I stood above it breathing
Living above what I lost
Sometimes I feel movement below
A memory knocking
I don’t answer
I buried myself to survive

LII

After the Last Goodbye Failed

I practiced goodbye for days
Words lined up carefully
Courage waited at the door
Then the moment arrived
My voice collapsed
Tears interrupted every sentence
You looked away
I looked down
Goodbye lost its meaning
It turned into silence
Silence turned into distance
Distance turned into forever
I replay that moment nightly
Trying different endings
None work
Goodbyes require two hearts
Mine stayed open
Yours already left
Now I say goodbye alone
To memories
To expectations
To who I was before you

LIII

I Am Still Here, Somehow

I don’t know how I survived
I just did
Days passed without permission
Nights dragged me through myself
I learned to function broken
To laugh lightly
To speak carefully
Pain learned my routine
It stopped surprising me
I stopped fighting it
People say time heals
Time only taught me endurance
I carry loss like a second spine
Invisible but heavy
I don’t chase happiness anymore
I accept quiet moments
Moments without hurting
If that is healing
Then this is it
I am still here
Somehow

LIV

Final Words

I reached the end without reaching peace
Some wounds chose to stay unnamed
Some memories refused forgiveness
I no longer search for what was lost
I only carry what remains
Pain changed its shape not its place
I learned how to breathe around it
How to live without answers
If you found yourself in these pages
Know this — you were never weak
You were feeling
And feeling deeply is survival

LV

Last Lines

This book does not end with healing
It ends with truth
Some hearts do not mend
They only grow quieter
Some stories do not close
They learn silence instead
I leave these words here
Not as closure
But as proof that pain was real

LVI

Dedication

To the nights that broke me quietly
To the tears that had no witness
To the love that left without explanation
To the version of me that did not survive
And to anyone who kept going
While falling apart inside
