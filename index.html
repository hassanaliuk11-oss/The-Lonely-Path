The halls of my mind echo with absence Footsteps that once mattered vanish I call, but only echoes respond Memories fade into dust and shadow And solitude reigns eternal
LXXV

Frosted Heart

The warmth we shared turns to frost Each beat echoes cold emptiness I search for heat in frozen memories Yet the chill persists unwavering And my soul trembles in winter’s grip
LXXVI

Vanishing Dreams

Dreams once vivid now dissolve in mist Hope retreats like a shadow at dusk I grasp for the fragments in vain Night swallows visions whole And I awaken to hollow despair
LXXVII

Empty Embrace

Arms reach for warmth that is gone A hollow space answers in return The echo of love haunts my chest I linger in the memory of touch And the void becomes my companion
LXXVIII

Shattered Dawn

Morning rises with a fractured light The day feels heavy on weary eyes Hope seems fragile as broken glass I walk through hours in silence And grief shadows every step
