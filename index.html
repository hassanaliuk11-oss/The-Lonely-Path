The Anatomy of Betrayal

Betrayal is not a sudden strike; it is a slow rot that begins in the quietest corners of a shared history. It is the realization that the hands you held to steady yourself were the same ones loosening the soil beneath your feet. Trust is a social contract, yet when it breaks, there is no court of law that can adjudicate the fracture of a soul.
