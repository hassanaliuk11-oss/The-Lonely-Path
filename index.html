Collection by Hassan Jatoi

THE LONELY
PATH

M M X X V
I

Silent Tears

The night falls heavy, stars refuse to shine, My eyes are rivers, flowing tears of mine. Each breath is hollow, each sigh a pain, Memories linger, like an endless chain. Shadows move softly across my room, Whispering your absence, igniting gloom. I reach for comfort, but find the air bare, A void that echoes, a wound laid bare. Your name is a whisper I cannot speak, It dances in silence, makes my knees weak. The walls remember, the floor knows my grief, Every corner of this house offers no relief.

II

Broken Heart

My heart lies shattered across cold stone, Each fragment whispers I am alone. Love once burned like fire in the night, Now embers fade, leaving only fright. I walk through the echoes of what was, Haunted by absence, bound by loss. Every smile I wore now feels like a lie, Laughter turned hollow, tears I cannot hide. Your shadow lingers in every hall, A silent echo, answering my call. Moonlight cuts through the window pane, And I shiver, remembering the pain.

III

Shadow of Memories

Shadows linger where you used to stand, Memories slipping like grains of sand. I touch the air, hoping for a trace, Only emptiness greets my embrace. Nights stretch endless, a river of dark, Every heartbeat echoes a hollow mark. Your laughter haunts the corners of mind, A cruel reminder of love left behind. I wander through rooms that remember your name, Each step is weighted with sorrow and shame. Dreams show your face then pull away, Leaving me trapped in another day.

IV

Lost Dreams

Dreams we built now lie in decay, Scattered fragments of a brighter day. I reach for hope but find only dust, Shattered trust, betrayal, and rust. Nights echo with silent screams, Torn by the loss of what might have been. Every step forward feels the same, Haunted by absence, drenched in pain. The moon hides behind a veil of sorrow, No promise shines for the hope of tomorrow. Tears water the soil of regret, Harvesting grief that I cannot forget.

V

Dark Night

The night swallows the last light of day, Leaving only darkness to guide my way. Stars blink faintly, indifferent above, While my soul searches for a lost love. Each shadow stretches across the floor, Echoing the pain that I cannot ignore. The wind carries whispers of your name, Filling the silence with sorrow and blame. I lie awake, haunted and torn, Remembering the nights when hope was born. The clock ticks slowly, mocking my despair, Every moment a reminder you’re not there.
