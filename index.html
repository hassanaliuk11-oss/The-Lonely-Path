THE LONELY
PATH

M M X X V
I

Silent Tears

The night falls heavy, stars refuse to shine, My eyes are rivers, flowing tears of mine. Each breath is hollow, each sigh a pain, Memories linger, like an endless chain. Shadows move softly across my room, Whispering your absence, igniting gloom. I reach for comfort, but find the air bare, A void that echoes, a wound laid bare. Your name is a whisper I cannot speak, It dances in silence, makes my knees weak. The walls remember, the floor knows my grief, Every corner of this house offers no relief.

II

Broken Heart

My heart lies shattered across cold stone, Each fragment whispers I am alone. Love once burned like fire in the night, Now embers fade, leaving only fright. I walk through the echoes of what was, Haunted by absence, bound by loss. Every smile I wore now feels like a lie, Laughter turned hollow, tears I cannot hide. Your shadow lingers in every hall, A silent echo, answering my call. Moonlight cuts through the window pane, And I shiver, remembering the pain.

III

Shadow of Memories

Shadows linger where you used to stand, Memories slipping like grains of sand. I touch the air, hoping for a trace, Only emptiness greets my embrace. Nights stretch endless, a river of dark, Every heartbeat echoes a hollow mark. Your laughter haunts the corners of mind, A cruel reminder of love left behind. I wander through rooms that remember your name, Each step is weighted with sorrow and shame. Dreams show your face then pull away, Leaving me trapped in another day.

IV

Lost Dreams

Dreams we built now lie in decay, Scattered fragments of a brighter day. I reach for hope but find only dust, Shattered trust, betrayal, and rust. Nights echo with silent screams, Torn by the loss of what might have been. Every step forward feels the same, Haunted by absence, drenched in pain. The moon hides behind a veil of sorrow, No promise shines for the hope of tomorrow. Tears water the soil of regret, Harvesting grief that I cannot forget.

V

Dark Night

The night swallows the last light of day, Leaving only darkness to guide my way. Stars blink faintly, indifferent above, While my soul searches for a lost love. Each shadow stretches across the floor, Echoing the pain that I cannot ignore. The wind carries whispers of your name, Filling the silence with sorrow and blame. I lie awake, haunted and torn, Remembering the nights when hope was born. The clock ticks slowly, mocking my despair, Every moment a reminder you’re not there.

II

Echoes of Waiting

The clock ticks slowly, a rhythm of pain, Searching for footsteps in the pouring rain. The door stays silent, the hallway is cold, A story of waiting, forever untold. I watch the horizon where the sun meets the sea, Hoping a breeze brings you back to me. The scent of your spirit still hangs in the hall, While shadows of memory dance on the wall. My soul is a desert, thirsting for grace, Lost in the absence of your smiling face. Every sunset is heavy, every dawn is a plea, To break these shackles and set my heart free. But the stars remain silent, the moon turns away, As I drift through the ghosts of another long day. The ink on the pages of our story has dried, Carrying the secrets I’ve buried inside.

III

Unspoken Words

A million words written, but none ever said, The paths of my heart are the ones I don't tread. I carry your reflection in the depths of my eyes, Under the weight of these vast, lonely skies. We walk in the same wind, we breathe the same air, Yet the distance between us is more than I bear. A touch that was fleeting, a glance that was brief, Is the root of my joy and the source of my grief. I build you a kingdom in the world of my sleep, With promises made that I’m destined to keep. The world hears my laughter, but never my soul, Missing the pieces that once made me whole. So I’ll stay in the shadows, a witness unseen, Lost in the spaces of what might have been. Loving you quietly, without sound or sign, Knowing you’re out there, but never quite mine.

II

Lost Dreams

Dreams we built now lie in decay, Scattered fragments of a brighter day. I reach for hope but find only dust, Shattered trust, betrayal, and rust. Nights echo with silent screams, Torn by the loss of what might have been. Every step forward feels the same, Haunted by absence, drenched in pain. The moon hides behind a veil of sorrow, No promise shines for the hope of tomorrow. Tears water the soil of regret, Harvesting grief that I cannot forget. Each morning arrives like a stone on my chest, Breathing feels heavy, sleep gives no rest. Shadows play on walls of my mind, Reminding me of the love I cannot find. I wander, lost, in a world turned gray, Searching for fragments that have slipped away. And yet, in this brokenness, I remain, Holding the dreams that will never sustain. Silent echoes of laughter and song, Remind me that what was, is forever gone.

III

Dark Night

The night swallows the last light of day, Leaving only darkness to guide my way. Stars blink faintly, indifferent above, While my soul searches for a lost love. Each shadow stretches across the floor, Echoing the pain that I cannot ignore. The wind carries whispers of your name, Filling the silence with sorrow and blame. I lie awake, haunted and torn, Remembering the nights when hope was born. The clock ticks slowly, mocking my despair, Every moment a reminder you’re not there. Tears trace paths down my weary face, Each drop a reminder of your warm embrace. The darkness presses, suffocating my chest, Offering no comfort, denying me rest. I speak to the night, but it answers with pain, A mirror of grief, an endless chain. Silence becomes my only friend tonight, Wrapping me tightly in sorrow’s quiet might. And as the night deepens, I remain alone, Holding memories that are carved in stone.

IV

Lonely Moments

I sit alone where shadows creep, The silence is thick, the memories deep. Laughter that once filled these walls is gone, Only whispers linger, soft and drawn. The moon peeks through with a pale cold light, Guiding my sorrow through the endless night. Each heartbeat echoes your absence near, Every sigh carries a trace of fear. I reach for warmth that cannot stay, Fading like dawn, slipping away. The world moves on, unknowing, unkind, While I am tethered to what I cannot find. Tears fall quietly, unnoticed by all, As I answer the grief that answers my call. Each passing hour deepens the ache, A lonely companion no joy can shake. And in this stillness, I quietly grieve, For the love I lost and cannot retrieve.

V

Nameless Sorrow

A sorrow with no name, no face, It drifts through me, leaving no trace. Silent as the wind, heavy as stone, I carry it everywhere, yet I am alone. The night whispers secrets I cannot bear, Filling the dark with despair. Each memory burns like a hidden flame, Reminding me of love and shame. I walk through shadows, searching for peace, But the pain lingers, it will not cease. The stars seem distant, indifferent above, I long for solace, I long for love. Tears fall quietly, unseen, unheard, Each one a fragment of a lost word. And in this quiet, endless and deep, I cradle the sorrow that refuses to sleep.

VI

Journey of Pain

I step along a road unkind, Leaving pieces of myself behind. Every mile echoes with a sigh, Every horizon mocks my cry. The wind cuts sharp against my face, I search for comfort, I search for grace. The shadows of memory cling to my heels, Dragging me back to wounds that never heal. I speak to the sky, but it remains mute, A mirror of sorrow, cold and absolute. Nights stretch long, the stars seem dim, A candle flickers, then grows dim. Yet still I walk, though weary and torn, Carrying the weight of a heart forlorn. This path is cruel, yet I must remain, A traveler lost in grief and pain.

VII

Empty Room

The room is silent, yet it breathes, Filled with echoes of what it perceives. Shadows stretch across the floor, Whispering of the one I adore. Every chair holds a memory tight, Every corner shivers with the night. I speak, but only the walls reply, Murmurs of absence, a soft goodbye. The moonlight touches objects cold, Reminding me of stories untold. Tears mark the floor, unseen by all, Each one a testament to my fall. I wander this room, a ghost in its space, Haunted by love I cannot replace. And as silence settles, heavy and deep, I lie awake while the room sleeps.

VIII

Complaint of the Heart

My heart whispers complaints to the wind, Of love departed, of grief pinned. It aches with burdens too heavy to name, Torn by passion, scorched by flame. I cry to the sky, but no answer comes, Only the echoes of lost drums. Memories pierce like a sharpened blade, Filling the night with the shadows they made. I reach for solace that cannot be found, Wandering lost, my soul unbound. Even laughter feels distant, a cruel jest, Each beat of my heart refuses rest. The weight of absence sits upon my chest, And in this suffering, I find no rest.

IX

Moments of Separation

Each second drags with the pain of you, Moments of separation, endless and true. I see your face in every fleeting shadow, Hear your voice in the wind’s quiet flow. The days are long, nights longer still, Carrying memories that refuse to heal. I reach for your hand, but grasp only air, A cruel reminder that you are not there. Tears fall silently, a river of ache, Tracing the loss that nothing can take. The stars watch on, cold and bright, Indifferent to my heart’s endless night. And I remain, a soul torn apart, Counting the pieces of my broken heart.
